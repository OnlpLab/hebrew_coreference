In this section you see all the expressions you need to label.
  • The current item is highlighted in yellow.
  • "Done" items are highlighted in grey and scratched out.
You can go to any expression by clicking the corresponding item.

Revisiting and editing "done" items

  • If you click a "done" item, it becomes "not done" again, and you can edit your answer.
  • If you want to make it "done" again, without editing, just click "Next".
  • The task is complete, when all the items are "done".

Items

In this section you see the entity clusters you created (therefore it's empty at the beginning). Once there are entity clusters in this section you can:
  1. remove an expression from a cluster by clicking [x];
  2. drag expressions from cluster to cluster.
If you hover over a cluster you'll see all its expressions highlighted in the text.
